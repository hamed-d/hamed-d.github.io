Hamed Damirchi

PhD researcher in machine learning · Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), Adelaide University

Adelaide, Australia

I’m a PhD researcher at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning, Adelaide University. My research makes large language models more reliable and cheaper to adapt: detecting reasoning errors from a model’s internal representations, fine-tuning with far fewer trainable parameters, and editing model behaviour more precisely. I’ve also built applied systems for electricity-demand forecasting, multi-agent LLM simulation for AI policy, and medical image analysis, and I teach LLM development to Master’s students.

What I work on

  • Reliable LLM reasoning: telling correct reasoning from errors using how a model’s internal representations change across layers.
  • Efficient adaptation and model editing: fine-tuning with fewer trainable parameters, and editing models through task vectors.
  • Applied AI systems: multi-agent LLM deliberation, demand forecasting, healthcare computer vision and robot localisation.
