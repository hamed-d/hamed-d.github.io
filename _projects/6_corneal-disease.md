**Role:** Tech Lead, ARAS Labs, 2021 to 2022. I led three engineers.

### The problem

The project aimed to detect corneal diseases, including keratoconus, from clinical data. This meant working with real hospital data and with several experts whose labels could disagree.

### What I did

- I led the technical and research work, directing the engineers on implementing the algorithms and on the pipeline from data processing to predictions.
- I coordinated data collection with ophthalmic surgeons and annotation by ophthalmologists.
- I designed the label aggregation that turns labels from several experts into a single ground-truth label.
- I proposed a weakly supervised learning approach to detection.
- I worked with the hospital to anonymise clinical data and set up an on-site processing environment, so identifiable data stayed on hospital premises.
