**Role:** Research Officer (RO4), Australian National University, Centre for Public Awareness of Science, 2024 to 2025. I worked in a four-person team with a social scientist, a psychology student and a tech lead.

### The problem

Policy on AI, and on other contested issues, involves many stakeholders with different interests. The project needed a way to simulate how those stakeholders would deliberate, with an emphasis on risk mitigation and governance.

### What I built

- I designed, implemented and deployed an LLM-based multi-agent framework for AI policy deliberation and risk mitigation.
- A retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system gives each agent context.
- With the psychology student, I designed the generated stakeholder personas, which capture each stakeholder's interests, needs, constraints and goals.
- LLM judges oversee deliberations and prevent repetitive loops. People can also take part as judges, as members of stakeholder teams or as independent stakeholder representatives.
- I established the evaluation and benchmarking frameworks.
- With the social scientist, I designed the simulations and prepared the data.

### Where it was applied

- Australian government AI-governance scenarios with about 400 national and international stakeholders.
- Murray River water disputes.
